What's on it.

Everything you check during the Charleston, and nothing you don't.

  • The pass order. Right, Across, Left. Then flip it.
  • The stop. Any one player can end it after the first Charleston.
  • Blind passes on passes 3 and 6, and the courtesy pass across.
  • Never pass a joker. Always three tiles.
  • What to keep and what to pass, for the moment you're stuck.
  • Large print is the same card with type about 20 percent bigger.

How to make one.

About five minutes, and it lasts all year.

  1. Pick your size.

    Standard fits the regular card. Large print fits the large print card.

  2. Print at 100%.

    Choose "Actual size", not "Fit to page", or it comes out too small. Card stock holds up best.

  3. Cut and slide it in.

    Cut on the marks and slide it behind your card in the sleeve, facing out.
