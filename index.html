How it works

Scoring as easy as 1, 2, 3.

Two points for a mahjong. One for a jokerless hand. One more if it was concealed. Reward whatever your table actually brags about, and leave out the rest.

Set your rules once

Two points for a mahjong, plus whatever your table rewards. Jokerless hands, concealed hands, back to back wins. Turn on only what you want.

Then just play

Tap who won and tap which bonuses applied. Points are added and the standings update themselves.

Crown a winner

Whoever tops the game night takes the title, and drinks from the good glass next time.

Play the long game

Every game night rolls into a season, so there is always something bigger on the line than tonight.

The season

Two ways to win

One board rewards turning up. The other rewards playing well when you do. Nobody is out of it just because life got busy.

Grand Champion

Most total points across the season. Every game night you play adds to your total, so showing up counts for something.

Hot Hand

Best average per game night. Miss a few weeks for work or travel and you are still right in the running.

Questions

The things people ask first.

Is this affiliated with the National Mah Jongg League?

No, it is not affiliated with the League in any way. It does support official NMJL scoring though, as one of two modes. Pick official and the values come off this year's card, with points moving from the payers to the winner. Pick casual, which is the default, and your table sets what a mahjong is worth and which bonuses count. You can switch whenever you like.

Does it use the NMJL card values?

It can. In official NMJL mode you enter the value printed on your own card for the hand that won, and the app handles the payments, including who pays double. It does not reproduce the card, its hand names or its values, so you still play from the card you bought.

Can our group make up its own table rules?

Yes, that is the whole idea. You set what a mahjong is worth and which bonuses count, and every game night is scored that way. Change them whenever your table decides to. If you are still working out what yours should be, there is a guide to American mahjong house rules on this site.

Do I need everyone to download the app?

No. One person keeps score for the table. Everyone else can be added as a player without an account, and anyone who wants to follow the standings on their own phone can use a free view-only code.

Do you have to play American mahjong for money?

No. Plenty of casual groups play for points and bragging rights only. The Mahj Report keeps a running score and a season title, so a night still means something without any money on the table.

What is a wall game in American mahjong?

A wall game is a hand where the tiles run out before anyone declares mahjong, so nobody wins it. The Mahj Report logs a wall game as a hand played with no winner, so it counts toward the night without giving anyone points.

How is American mahjong different from mahjong solitaire?

They are different games that share a name. Mahjong solitaire is the single-player tile-matching puzzle found in app stores and on free game sites. American mahjong is a four-player tile game played around a table, usually with the National Mah Jongg League card. This app is for the four-player game.

Where can I get a printable score sheet in the meantime?

There is a free printable American mahjong score sheet here. One page, six hands, a row for your own house rules, and no email required.

Why are there different spellings for mah jongg?

All of them are in use. The National Mah Jongg League and most American clubs write it as two words with two Gs, mah jongg. Newer brands and most players under fifty write mahjong as one word. Mah-jongg, majong and mahjongg all turn up too. They refer to the same family of games.
