What's inside

Six printable pages, plus a cover and a closing note.

  1. Getting Started

    Seven steps, about ten minutes, done once. Only the host needs anything.

  2. How Your Table Keeps Score

    The common standard in one column, and an empty column for your version. Write OFF next to anything your table does not play.

  3. Your House Rules

    The worksheet nobody ever fills in, which is why the same argument keeps happening. Every item is tagged NMJL or TABLE RULE, so you can see which ones have an official answer and which are yours to decide.

  4. Season Standings

    Eight game nights, twelve players, one page. Grand Champion and Hot Hand at the bottom.

  5. Teach a New Player in Ten Minutes

    Built for a room of twelve beginners and it works one on one just as well. The trick is what you leave out.

  6. Mahjong Night Bingo

    "I have nothing," then she wins. A wrong mahjong. The wall built wrong. Mark them off as they happen.
