Delete your account

And everything in it. Two ways to do it, both below.

The fastest way: in the app

You can delete your own account from inside The Mahj Report, without asking anyone. It happens immediately.

  • Open the app and go to Settings.
  • Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete my account.
  • Type delete my account to confirm, then tap the button.

We ask you to type the words on purpose. This cannot be undone, and there is no way for us to bring a deleted season back.

If you cannot use the app

If you have lost access to your device, forgotten which email you signed up with, or simply would rather not install anything, email us and we will do it for you.

hello@themahjreport.com

Tell us the email address on the account. We may ask you to confirm from that address, because otherwise anyone could delete anyone else's season.

We action deletion requests within 30 days, and usually within a few days. You do not need an account, a subscription, or the app installed to make a request.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign-in details.
  • Your groups, and the players, avatars and photos in them.
  • Every game night, hand, score and season standing.
  • Your date polls and the RSVPs people sent to them.
  • Your display name, email address and any preferences.

What we keep, and why

Two things outlive the account, and neither is personal to you:

  • Purchase and transaction records. If you ever subscribed, Apple or Google holds that receipt, not us, and we are required to keep basic records of the transaction for tax and accounting purposes. It does not contain your game data.
  • Anonymous, aggregated counts. Things like how many nights were scored in a month. These cannot be traced back to you or to anyone at your table.

Cancelling a subscription is a separate thing

Deleting your account does not cancel a paid subscription, because the subscription lives with your app store rather than with us. Cancel it yourself:

  • iPhone or iPad: Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions.
  • Android: the Play Store app, tap your profile picture, then Payments and subscriptions, then Subscriptions.

Do that first if you want to stop being billed, then delete the account.

Your players

The people at your table never had accounts. They only ever appear as a name you typed and, if you added one, a picture. Deleting your account removes all of that. If someone wants to be taken off your roster without you deleting everything, you can remove them individually from the Players tab, or email us and we will.

Questions about any of this are welcome at hello@themahjreport.com. Our full privacy policy has more detail on what we collect and why.
